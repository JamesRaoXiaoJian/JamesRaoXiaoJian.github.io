# James Rao · 饶小建

[中文](README.md) · [English](README_EN.md)

Hi, I’m James. I study computer science at Shenzhen University, and these days I mostly work on robots.

That usually means collecting data, running simulations, and trying models on real arms and dexterous hands. I’m interested in what actually happens when a model gets to a real robot, including the small data and control problems that are easy to overlook.

A few things I’m working on:

- [sim2real_g1](https://github.com/JamesRaoXiaoJian/sim2real_g1): deploying and evaluating policy models on G1’s arms and dexterous hands.
- [TransVTLA-RealDataCollect](https://github.com/JamesRaoXiaoJian/TransVTLA-RealDataCollect): collecting RGB-D, tactile, and robot-state data together and preparing it for training.
- [LEAP-DexRetarget](https://github.com/JamesRaoXiaoJian/LEAP-DexRetarget): mapping human hand motions to robot hands. Currently working on retargeting and simulation checks.

I also built [SV_Soul](https://github.com/JamesRaoXiaoJian/SV_Soul), an AI dialogue mod for Stardew Valley NPCs with memory of past conversations and awareness of the game state.

Open an issue for project questions, or say hello about robots, data, or AI in games.
